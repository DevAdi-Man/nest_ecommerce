# Bazaar — Open Basket identity

Approved by the user: **H — Open Basket**. The lowercase b is the identifier; its open, rounded construction gives the system a less severe visual voice. The basket interpretation is a design metaphor, not a literal cart pictogram. Use the locked logo assets rather than retyping the name or rebuilding its proportions.

## Palette roles

- Deep teal `#0D3D3A`: navigation and brand anchor; a darker base for readable light text.
- Amber `#FFC47A`: primary shopping actions and the reversed symbol; its warm/cool contrast separates action from structural navigation.
- Ivory `#FAF8F4`: canvas; white `#FFFFFF`: content surfaces. The quiet backgrounds keep photographic products visually prominent.
- Ink `#162F2C` and secondary text `#546561`: information hierarchy without relying on light, low-contrast labels.
- Link teal `#00685F`, success `#176846`, danger `#B32632`: separate interaction and feedback roles. Keep labels/icons alongside status colour.

These are deliberate hierarchy and harmony choices, not a claim that a colour universally produces a psychological response. Use amber selectively; do not turn every panel into an accent surface. Seven principal text/background combinations pass 4.5:1; exact ratios are in `contrast-audit.json`.

## Assets

`assets/brand/bazaar-logo-primary.svg`, `bazaar-logo-reversed.svg` and `bazaar-logo-mono.svg` are outlined vector lockups. `bazaar-symbol.svg` is the standalone mark. The header and 16/24/32/64 px files have real SVG dimensions, so the native importer scales the artwork rather than clipping an oversized drawing.

Keep clear space at least one quarter of the symbol height. Do not stretch the asset, add an icon container, change symbol/wordmark spacing, or use the reversed variant on a light background.
